Product: Ownership (formal: Ownership Arc Naming Service). Separate identity from the Lodestar Family Office mark.

## The mark

Three concepts in exploration: Helm (steer your own ship), Anchor (holds fast), Key (own your keys).
Decision pending — Douglas picks.

## Usage

- Primary lockup: wordmark on white, no border. The symbol lives inside the word (Exxon-style) — currently exploring the "O" as keyring, brand-ring, and gold ring.
- One-color: navy on light, white on navy (reversed applications).
- Wordmark: "Ownership", bold grotesque, sentence case.

## Rules (standard)

- Exactly the two spot colors. No gradients, no shading, no extra colors.
- No drop shadows, no rotation, no stretching, no recoloring.
- Clear space on all sides = cap-height of the "O" in the wordmark.
- Minimum size: 24px digital / 8mm print.

## Wordplay (in exploration, Douglas's catch)

"OWNERSHIP" contains "OWNER" and "HIP" (inside "ship") — owners are hip. Color-split variants reveal it: OWNERS in the primary spot color, HIP in the accent.

## Logo (DECIDED 2026-10-03, Douglas: "Beautiful")

Primary mark: the bare "Ownership" wordmark alone. The "ARC NAMING SERVICE" descriptor lockup is a secondary formal variant — letterhead and formal documents only (Douglas: the descriptor "doesn't earn its place" in the primary mark).

"Owners" in bold yellow gold (capital O as a geometric gold ring holding a navy ship's helm) + "hip" in lowercase bold navy. Mixed-case wordmark on white, no border. OWNER/HIP color-split reveals the wordplay.
- Typeface: bold high-contrast bracketed serif with narrow (condensed) proportions — v2 uses Bitter Bold (OFL) with 0.70 horizontal condense baked into outlines, measured against the approved render.
- Outline treatment: thin navy outline OUTSIDE the gold letters/O. The navy "hip" letters are solid (inline removed 2026-10-03, Douglas). No navy outline on the inside edge of the O ring.
- ™ lockup (standard practice, confirmed 2026-10-03): the ™ rides with the logo — upper-right superscript in navy, tucked against the final "p". All distributed use-files (JPG/PNG) carry it: `vector/ownership-logo-with-tm.jpg` is the social-ready master. The vector master (`ownership-logo-v2.svg`) stays clean — USPTO design-mark drawings exclude ™. Tiny circular avatars (profile pics): clean mark, ™ carried in the bio/profile text instead. Consistent use everywhere: site header, docs, socials. Never ® (registered-only).
- Social kit (`brand/social/`, built 2026-10-03): `avatar-o-512.png` (O-ring helm icon, circle-crop safe — the profile pic), `banner-x-1500x500.png` (X header), `banner-discord-960x540.png` (Discord server banner). All white background, ™ on the banners.
- Helm: proper 8-spoke ship's wheel with handles and hub, navy, centered in the O's counter (matches the approved render).
- O spec: geometric ring, thick gold band (~20% of diameter), vertically centered on the x-height band — extends above and below the letters' baseline (per the approved render).
